Services

What Olean IT takes care of

Most calls are one of these four. If yours isn't on the list, ask anyway.

Business

IT support

Slow PCs, printers that won't print, email that won't send, a new hire who needs a working laptop. Help when something stops, on site or remote.

Monthly

Managed IT plans

A flat monthly fee for the ongoing care of your office's computers, accounts and network, so IT becomes a line in the budget and not a surprise.

Network

Networks and Wi-Fi

Dead spots, slow speeds and tangled closets. Router and switch setup, cabling, guest Wi-Fi, and a network that was laid out on purpose.

Bench

Phone repair

Cracked screens, worn-out batteries and charging ports that gave up. Repairs for common phones, done locally.

How it works

From "it's broken" to working again

  1. Say what's wrong

    Send an email describing what's happening, in your own words. No ticket portal and no phone tree.

  2. Hear back with a plan

    You're told what it most likely is and whether it needs a visit or can be handled remotely.

  3. Fixed and written down

    The problem gets fixed, and you get a plain note of what was done so the next visit starts ahead.

One technician, based in Olean

Olean IT is run by Levi Johnson. When you ask for help, he's the person who answers, the person who shows up, and the person who remembers how your office is set up.

The work covers Olean and the surrounding Western New York area, with remote support for anything that doesn't need hands on the hardware.

Security is part of the job

Olean IT contributes attack reports to AbuseIPDB, a public database that networks around the world use to block abusive addresses.
